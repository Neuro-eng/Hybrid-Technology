**How to use these:** Take the [Diagnostic Quiz](diagnostic-quiz.html) → work through Exams 1–9 by topic to shore up weak areas → take Exam 10 as a mixed review → finish with the [65-question Full-Length Mock](practice-exam-11-full-mock.html) timed to 90 minutes, to simulate real exam conditions.

**Is this enough to pass?** These 111 questions cover every domain and are a strong practice foundation, but no single question bank guarantees a pass. For best results, pair this with: the [official AWS exam guide](https://aws.amazon.com/certification/certified-cloud-practitioner/), AWS Skill Builder's free digital training, and hands-on time in the AWS Free Tier console. Repetition across multiple sources — not just one set of practice questions — is what closes knowledge gaps most reliably.

**Content freshness:** The live "Live AWS Updates" ticker on the homepage always shows current AWS announcements. The practice questions themselves are reviewed periodically against AWS's official exam guide (see the version note in `README.md`) — this isn't fully automatic, since blindly auto-rewriting exam questions from news headlines risks introducing inaccurate answers without human review. To refresh the question bank, come back anytime and ask for a content check against the latest exam guide.
